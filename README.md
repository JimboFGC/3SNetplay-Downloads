# 3SHub downloads

**3SHub**, formerly 3SNetplay, is a Windows x64 desktop client for 3rd Strike with native FBNeo emulation and GGPO rollback.

[Download the Windows launcher](https://github.com/JimboFGC/3SHub-Downloads/releases/download/alpha/3SNetplay-Launcher.zip) | [Website and roadmap](https://3shub-website-production.up.railway.app/) | [Release notes](https://github.com/JimboFGC/3SHub-Downloads/releases/tag/alpha)

## Get started

1. Extract the launcher ZIP and open **Play 3SHub.cmd**.
2. Select your compatible local ROMs and configure your controller.
3. Use your alpha invitation to register, then challenge a player or create/join a private room.

Windows 10/11 x64 and Microsoft Edge WebView2 are required. No ROMs are provided, downloaded or uploaded. Keep the launcher folder for future signed updates. Fully close the game and app before reopening the launcher to update; local settings are preserved.

## Available in the alpha

- Public lobby chat, direct challenges, GIFs and local Favorites.
- Private Normal rooms with member challenges and simultaneous matches.
- Private KOTH rooms with custom invite codes, FT1/2/3/5, queues and winner character lock with free Super Art selection.
- Room chat, member-only spectators and host controls.
- Live spectating, viewer counts and customizable overlays.
- Effie Training, Trials with saved local clears and Offline Arcade.
- Shared settings, CRT filters, runahead and music mute.
- Update alerts, password changes and automatic alpha diagnostics with an opt-out.

**Current build: 011957f1.** Outdated clients cannot start new online sessions or enter the KOTH queue. Waiting players are checked before pairing; matches already underway may finish. The release also fixes closed-window reuse and defers update probes during gameplay.

Intermediate/Expert parry trials remain experimental. Saved replays, Community Palmods, ranked and tournament tools are planned.

## Alpha limits

Shared stutter/audio reports, controller coverage and longer multi-machine sessions remain under investigation. Current KOTH transitions are acceptable for the alpha; further speed improvements will come later. Private rooms clear on service restart and expire after 15 minutes empty.

Direct P2P exposes your network address to your opponent. Router compatibility remains under testing; experimental relay fallback does not guarantee connectivity. Windows publisher signing (Authenticode) is not present. Signed update manifests and archive hashes provide a separate integrity check; obtain the initial launcher here.
